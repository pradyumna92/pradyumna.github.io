Inspired by ISTQB CTFL v4.0.1, sections 5.1.3, 5.2 and 5.3. This is an educational simulation, not an ISTQB-mandated checklist or an actual approval system. Targets and non-waivable gates below are example team policy.

01 / ObjectivesAssess the identified product risks on the release candidate being reviewed.
02 / Coverage & executionExample mandatory gate: 100% of identified critical journeys.

Planned = passed + failed + blocked + not run. Execution = (passed + failed) / planned. Blocked tests are not completed executions.
03 / DefectsIncludes critical defects and any other severity classified as a release blocker. Cannot be waived in this demo.
04 / Residual riskUse fictional information. This simulation stays in your browser and does not record approvals. Acceptance cannot override mandatory gates.
05 / Release authorizationOperational readiness and final approval are additional release controls, distinct from QA test completion.
